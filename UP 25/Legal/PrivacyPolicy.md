# Privacy Policy — 25-40

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

25-40 ("the App") is a card game published by Atiko Labs ("we", "us", "our"). This policy covers the Apple mobile version and the macOS, Windows, and Steam Deck versions distributed through Steam.

## 1. The short version

You can play 25-40 without an account and without sending personal data to us. The Steam version is offline-first, does not sign in to our servers, and does not send us your Steam identity. The Apple version can optionally use Game Center to keep statistics across devices. We do not sell data, track you across apps or websites, show advertising, or use analytics SDKs.

## 2. Local data

The App stores the following data on your device:

- sound and language preferences
- tutorial completion
- finished-game and win counts
- a local progress file

This data is not transmitted to us. Removing the App and its local data removes it. If you enable Steam Cloud in the Steam client, Valve may sync the progress file under Valve's own terms and privacy policy.

## 3. Steam version

The current Steam build does not use Steamworks identity, achievements, leaderboards, telemetry, or our Supabase backend. We do not receive your Steam ID, Steam display name, purchase history, or gameplay data from the App.

Steam itself is operated by Valve. The Steam client and store may process account, purchase, device, crash, and usage information independently under Valve's Privacy Policy (https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). Atiko Labs does not control that platform processing.

## 4. Optional Game Center account on Apple devices

If you choose to sign in on the Apple version, Game Center provides:

- Game Center team player ID, used to identify your account
- Game Center display name, shown at the table and in standings

We also store score, finishing position, win status, and timestamp. We do not receive your real email address. A synthetic, non-deliverable identifier is generated for our backend. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. What we do not collect

The App does not collect advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking data, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, health data, browsing history, or data for third-party advertising or analytics.

## 6. Use and processors

Data from an optional Apple account is used only to authenticate the player, display a name, and keep game statistics. It is processed by Supabase in the EU (eu-central-1) and by Apple Game Center. We do not profile players, make automated decisions about them, or sell or rent personal information.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Apple-version account data is kept while the account exists. You can permanently remove the profile and associated game results from Settings → Account management → Delete account. The Apple Game Center account is unaffected. Provider infrastructure backups age out according to the provider's backup lifecycle.

The Steam version creates no account with us, so there is no server account to delete. Local progress can be removed by deleting the App's data; Steam Cloud copies, if enabled by you, are managed through Steam.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, port, restrict, delete, or object to processing of personal data. Contact us to exercise those rights.

## 9. Children

25-40 contains no gambling, wagering, simulated gambling, chat, or user-to-user communication. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Security and international transfers

Apple-version network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. Row-level database security restricts a signed-in player to their own profile and results. Account deletion uses a privileged server-side function whose key is never included in the App. Apple-version account data is processed in the European Union and may be transferred there from your location.

## 12. Changes

We will update this policy before changing what the App collects or adding Steamworks identity, online statistics, achievements, or leaderboards.
